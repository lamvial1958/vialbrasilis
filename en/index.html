The problem

The accounts show a profit, the bank shows something else

💰
Cash tied up

Customers paying late, idle inventory, suppliers to pay before you collect: the money exists, but not where you need it.

📉
Shrinking margins

Revenue grows but margin falls: service, payroll or raw-material costs rise faster than sales, and nobody notices in time.

🧭
No clear priority

Many numbers, few decisions. You need to know what to do first, who does it and what it is worth in euros.

How it works

From your accounts to an action plan, in four steps

1
Data

You send me the financial statements (2–3 years) and the sales, receivables and inventory data.

2
Analysis

I check that the figures add up and calculate the indicators with your sector's thresholds.

3
Plan

I identify the 3–5 actions that release the most cash or margin, with the estimated effect in euros.

4
Meeting

I present the report and we simulate the scenarios together, at your premises or by video call.

What you get, confidentiality and details

Why it is different

Every sector has its own limits

A pharmacy, a factory and a software company are not judged by the same thresholds. The analysis distinguishes eight types of company (industry, trade, services, transport, construction, agriculture, healthcare, regulated services) and reads the indicators accordingly, starting from the ATECO code. Every figure is traceable: I state what is given, what is calculated and what is estimated.
