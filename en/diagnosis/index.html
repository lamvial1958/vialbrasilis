Fast cash and margin diagnostics for companies

Where is your company's money stuck? In a few weeks you will see it, in euros.

I start from the last 2 or 3 years of financial statements and, in about two weeks, show you where cash gets stuck (customers, inventory, suppliers), whether your margins are healthy for your sector and what to do first, with the estimated impact in euros.

What you get

  • Indicator traffic lights with different thresholds by type of company (industry, trade, services, transport, construction, agriculture, healthcare, regulated services).
  • Cash cycle explained: how many days money stays tied up in receivables, inventory and supplier payables.
  • CNDCEC early-warning ratios (art. 13 of the Italian Crisis Code) as an early signal, not as legal advice.
  • 30-, 60- and 90-day plan with actions in order of value and effort, and a simulator showing the effect of each.
  • Written report (in Italian) and a presentation meeting.

How it works

  • 1. Data. You send me the financial statements (2–3 years) and the sales, receivables and inventory data.
  • 2. Analysis. I check that the figures add up and calculate the indicators for your sector.
  • 3. Plan. I identify the 3–5 actions that release the most cash or margin.
  • 4. Meeting. I present the report and we simulate the scenarios together, at your premises or by video call.

Why it is different

  • Reading by sector: a pharmacy, a factory and a software company are not judged by the same limits.
  • Tested on real public financial statements of Italian companies in different sectors.
  • Every figure is traceable: I state what is given, what is calculated and what is estimated.

Confidentiality

  • Non-disclosure agreement signed first, before I receive any data.
  • Data stay only on my computer, with an encrypted disk and no cloud; they are not shared with third parties.
  • At the end of the engagement I delete everything (data and backups) and confirm in writing.
  • If you consent, I use the results to improve the tool only in anonymous form: ratios and percentages, with no names, amounts or clients. You see beforehand what is kept. Declining does not change the service.

Pricing

All prices exclude VAT. Travel costs, if needed, are extra and agreed in advance. Payment: 50% on signing and 50% on delivery of the report.

  • Diagnostics: € 2,200. Financial statements plus sales, receivables and inventory data, simulator, report and two meetings.
  • Quarterly follow-up: € 2,200 per quarter, after the diagnostics. Same effort and same fee as the diagnostics (see below).

Quarterly follow-up: how it works

It is optional and you choose it after seeing the diagnostics. If you do not see results, you do not renew it.

  • What each quarter includes: monthly data updates, a check of the plan's results, an indicators report and a review meeting, at your premises or by video call.
  • Fee: € 2,200 per quarter, equal to the diagnostics, because the work is the same. VAT excluded; invoiced at the start of the quarter.
  • Stopping: you can stop at the end of any quarter with written notice of at least 15 business days before it ends; if notice arrives later, the next quarter is due. Everything, including start and stop, is done in writing.
  • Resuming: after a stop, resuming is the same as a new engagement: the data have been deleted and the baseline is outdated, so we restart with a new diagnostics at the fee then in force.
  • Who decides: the actions are decided and carried out by the client. I do not guarantee results: the simulation is a working assumption, not a forecast.
