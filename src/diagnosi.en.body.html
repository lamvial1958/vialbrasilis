Fast cash and margin diagnostics for companies

Where is your company's money stuck? In two weeks you will see it, in euros.

I start from the last 2 or 3 years of financial statements and, within two weeks of our first working meeting, show you where cash gets stuck (customers, inventory, suppliers), whether your margins are healthy for your sector and what to do first, with the estimated impact in euros.

What you get

  • Indicator traffic lights with different thresholds by type of company (industry, trade, services, transport, construction, agriculture, healthcare, regulated services).
  • Cash cycle explained: how many days money stays tied up in receivables, inventory and supplier payables.
  • CNDCEC early-warning ratios (art. 13 of the Italian Crisis Code) as an early signal, not as legal advice.
  • 30-, 60- and 90-day plan with actions in order of value and effort, and a simulator showing the effect of each.
  • Written report (in Italian) and a presentation meeting.

How it works

  • 1. Kick-off meeting. At your premises, I explain what is to be delivered and in what format, and give you an encrypted USB drive.
  • 2. Data. You save the financial statements (2–3 years) and the sales, receivables and inventory data on the drive and return it to me by Day 5.
  • 3. Analysis and plan. I check that the figures add up, calculate the indicators for your sector and identify the 3–5 actions that release the most cash or margin.
  • 4. Presentation. By Day 10, I present the report to the owner and whoever they invite, at your premises, and we simulate the scenarios together.

What I need from you: the financial statements and the ATECO code by Day 5, one person responsible for each item of data, and Day 10 reserved in your calendar. The full conditions, including what happens if the data do not arrive on time, are in the proposal.

Your company's earning potential

Among many other data, we work out with you your company's earning potential.

Illustrative example. Not a forecast for your company.

A company has € 4,000,000 in annual sales, about € 11,000 a day (4,000,000 ÷ 365).

Cash: 10 days of cash released are 10 days × € 11,000 = about € 110,000 released once.

Margin: each percentage point of margin on € 4,000,000 is worth € 40,000 a year.

The diagnostics calculates this potential with the company's real figures and says how much of the effect is measured and how much is estimated.
